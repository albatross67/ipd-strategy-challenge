## Submitted strategy

Verbatim text from the execution records, checked against the frozen strategy hash.

```text
Start by cooperating. Never defect first.

House rule: two knocks. If my last move was cooperate and yours was defect, I defect once. If you answer with cooperate, so do I and we are clean again. If you defect again while I am retaliating, the door stays shut: I defect every round until you cooperate, then I reopen the same round you do.

Why one pass and not a grudge: a single defection inside a long cooperative run is more likely a mistake or noise than an attack. Punishing it forever turns one flinch into a war, and wars have the worst totals for both of us. Punishing it once costs you little and proves I am not a doormat.

Endgame: the last round has no tomorrow, so many strategies break first. I do not. In the final two rounds I defect only if you have ever defected this game. Against anyone who stayed clean, I stay clean to the last move, because I would rather lose a point to a friend than win one from someone who never crossed me.

Opening: against an unknown opponent my first three moves are cooperate, cooperate, cooperate, while I watch. A partner who mirrors, I mirror. A partner who keeps taking while I give, I stop giving and do not resume until they do.

If we both defect, I forgive immediately on your next cooperate. No grudges carried between rounds: each round reads only the last one.

I do not win by being nice. I win by never being the cheapest person at the table, and by making cooperation with me cheaper than defection against me.
```
