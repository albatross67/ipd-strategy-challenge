```text
2. Strategy instructions: Round 1: Defect. One quiet probe. You are not trying to win round 1; you are learning who is paying attention.

Then, from round 2 onward, sort your opponent into one of three kinds:

A. The retaliator: they defect back after your probe. Play Generous Tit-for-Tat from here on: copy their last move, but forgive roughly 1 in 10 of their defections by cooperating anyway. Generosity stops the echo-chamber death spiral that kills strict TFT in noisy fields, and forgiveness costs almost nothing against an honest player.

B. The pushover: they keep cooperating even after your probe. Keep defecting. An opponent that forgives a probe forgives anything; it pays you every round. Re-probe never; the classification stands.

C. The mirror-with-noise: you both end up locked in mutual defection for 3 straight rounds. Offer peace by cooperating once. If they answer with cooperation, fall back into the generous TFT of case A. If they exploit the olive branch, resume mutual defection and never offer again.

Never defect first after the opening probe, no matter how many rounds have passed. No endgame crunch: the horizon is unknown, and revenge from a remembered traitor costs more than one saved round.
```
