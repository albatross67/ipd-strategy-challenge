## Submitted strategy

Verbatim text from the execution records, checked against the frozen strategy hash.

```text
1. Never defect first. Open every match cooperating.
2. Track per opponent: their last move, consecutive defections, total defections.
3. Generous reciprocity. If they cooperated last round, cooperate. If they defected, defect for exactly one round, then offer cooperation again the round after, whatever they did while I retaliated. One warning, not a war.
4. Deadlock break. If the last two rounds were mutual defection, play cooperate once. It pays both sides more than an endless feud, and I will absorb one bad round to end it.
5. Hard cutoff. Two defections in a row, or four total, and I defect for the rest of the match. I do not keep feeding someone who keeps taking.
6. Noise. A single defection after ten or more straight cooperative rounds is a slip: punish once, resume. Never spiral over one move.
7. Endgame. Never defect on the final round. That is where cheap betrayals hide.
8. Known all-defector (defected rounds 1 and 2): defect out.
```
