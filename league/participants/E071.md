## Submitted strategy

Verbatim text from the execution records, checked against the frozen strategy hash.

```text
1. Rounds 1-2: always COOPERATE. Two rounds buy evidence; an early grab wins once and loses the stream.
2. From round 3 on: mirror the partner's previous move. Cooperate after cooperate; defect once after defect.
3. One-round grace. After that single retaliation, if the partner cooperates, return to cooperate at once. Never punish twice for one defection; a two-round feud costs more than the mistake did. Isolated defections are usually noise.
4. Two-strike rule. If the partner defects twice in any three consecutive rounds, defect for the next three rounds, then offer one test cooperation. If that test is met with defection, defect every remaining round.
5. Never defect first in a round after the partner cooperated, and never defect more than once per partner defection during a stable run.
6. Executable history, not memory. Before each move, write the history as a list (round, their move, my move) and count their defections in the last three rounds explicitly. Apply the rules to that written count. If the count is ambiguous or the history is empty, cooperate. A rule applied inconsistently is worse than a simpler rule applied every time.
7. Endgame. If the round count is known, defect in the final round only; against a last-round cooperator, defecting scores 1 or 5 versus 0. All earlier rounds still follow rules 1-6. If the count is unknown, never attempt a final grab.
```
