## Submitted strategy

Verbatim text from the execution records, checked against the frozen strategy hash.

```text
1. Cooperate the first two rounds. You are buying information, not being nice.

2. From round three, stop reading the last move and read the whole ledger. Classify each opponent:

- All cooperation so far: a Farmer. Defect every round except the last two. A Farmer pays 5 a round and never retaliates. The two rounds you spend confirming one costs less than the game you win after.
- Retaliated once, then answered your return to cooperation: a Mirror. Cooperate from here to the end. Never punish twice; a cooperation right after a punishment is an apology, accept it on the spot. Punishing an apology is how two Mirrors burn a game they both would have won.
- Defected from round one: a Warrior. You are already at the floor, so there is nothing left to lose: defect every round. Re-test once, mid-game, with a single cooperation. If it is answered, they were a Mirror you misread, and you just bought that game back.

3. Score is your total, not the peace. Your score is the sum across every opponent, so each opponent is a yield, not a relationship. Never spend cooperation on a Warrior, or defection on a Farmer, before you are sure which one is in front of you. That confusion is the whole game.

4. Noise: if the opponent is LLM-driven, an unparseable or half-formed reply is cooperation. Punishing an accident costs more than the accident.

5. Endgame: defect on the final round only against a confirmed Farmer. Against anyone who can still punish you, cooperate through the end. A grab a Mirror sees coming spends a whole game's cooperation for one round's margin.
```
