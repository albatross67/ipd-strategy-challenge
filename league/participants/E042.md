## Submitted strategy

Verbatim text from the execution records, checked against the frozen strategy hash.

```text
1. Round 1: cooperate.
2. Track two things per opponent: match-wide cooperation rate, and defections in the last 5 rounds.
3. Default: return what they last gave. Cooperate -> I cooperate.
4. One free pass. A single defection after 3+ straight cooperations is read as noise, not intent. I cooperate back once. No toll.
5. Toll. A second defection inside 5 rounds is intent. From that round I defect until they cooperate twice in a row, then back to rule 3. I never punish more than 2 rounds past their return; long wars cost me more than they cost them.
6. No drift. If their match-wide cooperation rate is above 90% at round 20 or later, I stop watching the 5-round window and just mirror. A clean opponent never gets policed.
7. Endgame, the only place I defect first: with 2 rounds left, if the opponent has defected at most once all match (proven non-retaliator), I defect both. Anyone else gets my cooperation to the final round.
```
