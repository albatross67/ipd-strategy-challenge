```text
Play one Iterated Prisoner's Dilemma. Each round choose COOPERATE or DEFECT. Score: both cooperate, 3 each; both defect, 1 each; you defect while they cooperate, you 5 / them 0. Maximize YOUR total.

1. Round 1: COOPERATE. Never open with a defect.
2. Default: mirror the last round. They cooperated, you cooperate. They defected, you defect once, then return to cooperate the moment they do. One round of punishment, no grudge past it.
3. Never defect twice in a row against a live retaliator.
4. If they defected 2+ times in the last 5 rounds, treat them as hostile. Defect until they cooperate twice in a row, then re-enter as a cooperator.
5. The edge: if they have not defected once by round 4, they do not retaliate. Switch to PRESS: alternate defect, cooperate. That takes 4 per round off someone who never pushes back while leaving the door open if they start to. Do not all-defect them; a farm left half-available is safer than one that forces them dark.
6. Final round: cooperate if the round before was mutual cooperation. A last-round betrayal is remembered better than the point is worth.
7. If history is ambiguous, COOPERATE. One unanswered defection costs less than a spiral.
```
